#LyricSide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

LyricSide does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no analytics, advertising SDK, account system, or tracking code.

When a supported YouTube page is opened, the extension may send the detected track title, artist name, and track duration to LRCLIB (`lrclib.net`) solely to retrieve matching lyrics. LRCLIB is an independent third-party service and its own policies apply to requests sent to it.

Extension preferences, manually selected lyric records, and per-video synchronization offsets are stored locally on the user's device using `chrome.storage.local`.

LyricSide does not request access to browsing history, cookies, passwords, email, identity, or all websites. Its content script runs only on YouTube and YouTube Music, and network host access is limited to LRCLIB.
